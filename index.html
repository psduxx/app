Consulting

Mining engineering for open-cut operations

Our core work is engineering consulting for the main open-cut mining methods, from mid-term designs to day-to-day support of the site team.

01 · dragline

Dragline

Strip and pit designs that work for the dragline, its pads and its spoil room.

  • Strip, key cut and extended bench design
  • Pad and bench levels, dig sequences
  • Spoil room and rehandle checks
  • Volumes by strip and block
02 · d&b

Drill & blast

Drill and blast designs matched to the mine plan and the rock.

  • Pattern design: burden, spacing and hole depth
  • Cast and through-seam blast design
  • Powder factor and blast volumes
  • Drill plans and blast reconciliation
03 · truck & shovel

Truck & shovel

Pit, ramp and dump designs for truck and shovel fleets.

  • Pit shells, benches, ramps and haul roads
  • Waste dump and prestrip designs
  • Cut/fill volumes by block or polygon
  • Sequencing support for the schedule
04 · mid-term

Mid-term design

Practical designs and plans for the next few months to two years.

  • Mid-term pit, strip and dump designs
  • Sections, toes and crests for review
  • Volume reconciliation against design
  • Budget and forecast support
