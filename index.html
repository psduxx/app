06

FAQ

6 answers
Where is our data stored?

Where you choose. We can host in Australia on request, and we'll document where each part of the system stores and processes data, including any third-party AI services.

Who owns the software you build?

You do. Once a custom project is paid for, the code and IP are yours. Ready-made products are licensed to you, unless you choose a source-code buy-out.

Is AI right for our business?

Sometimes. AI suits some tasks, like searching documents, drafting and classifying, and is a poor fit for others. In the free consult we'll give you a straight answer, including when conventional software or no change is the better option.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

We work remotely across Australia, Monday to Friday AEST. If you think an on-site visit would help, raise it in the consult and we'll discuss whether it's practical.

How do payments and GST work?

All quotes are in AUD and include GST. Custom projects are paid by milestone; retainers are billed monthly. Accounts questions go to account@dumc.com.au.

Will you keep our information confidential?

Yes. We can sign an NDA before you share anything sensitive, we follow the Australian Privacy Principles voluntarily, and we use your data only for your project.
