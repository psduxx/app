Pit, ramp and dump designs worked from your survey and geological surfaces.

02 · dozer

Dozer push design & costing

Spoil landforms generated from your working topo and cut target, with the volumes balanced.

  • Push face, spoil batter and endwall design
  • Push sections along the push bearing
  • Production-curve cost and dozer-hour estimates
  • Design surfaces exported for your mine software
03 · water

Pit water & storm studies

How design storms fill and spill across your pit, and what it takes to pump it out.

  • BoM IFD design storms with ARR patterns
  • Ponding, spill paths and flow paths
  • Peak storage and time to dewater
  • Pump sizing and scenarios

We built our own 3D mine design tool so studies are faster and repeatable. It works with the files your site already uses, and every result can be checked in section and exported.

Design tools
